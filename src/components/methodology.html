Methodology

Five stages. One clear path forward.

Our methodology brings structure to what can otherwise be a complicated funding journey — a repeatable process built for disciplined execution.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    Understand before we recommend — eligibility, financial structure, business model, operational position, documentation and policy & scheme compatibility.

  2. 02

    Financial architecture

    Structure the opportunity — debt-equity considerations, subsidy opportunities, funding mix, capital requirement and documentation architecture.

  3. 03

    Submission

    Prepare with precision — application preparation, DPR and supporting documents, compliance readiness and submission management.

  4. 04

    Approval

    Stay with the process — query management, stakeholder coordination, application follow-up and process monitoring.

  5. 05

    Disbursement

    From sanction to capital — sanction coordination, capital receipt and ongoing compliance support.

Honest expectations. Outcomes depend on eligibility, scheme criteria, documentation and the institutions’ own review. We are transparent about what is realistic for your case before you proceed — we do not guarantee approvals.
