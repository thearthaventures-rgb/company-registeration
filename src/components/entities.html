What type of company should you register?

Each structure balances ownership, liability and compliance differently. Here is a plain-language view of the common options.

Popular for startups

Private Limited Company

Suitable for startups and growing businesses that plan to hire, sign contracts or raise investment.

  • Separate legal entity
  • Limited liability
  • Equity is fundable and transferable

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Suitable for professional services and partner-managed businesses that want formal structure with lighter compliance.

  • Partner-based management
  • Limited liability
  • Lighter statutory compliance

One Person Company (OPC)

Suitable for solo founders who want a formal company without a co-founder requirement.

  • Single member
  • Limited liability
  • Incorporated identity

Partnership Firm

Suitable for small teams who want to share ownership and profits without full incorporation.

  • Shared ownership and profits
  • Unincorporated, simple to run
  • Partners generally bear personal liability

Sole Proprietorship

For individuals running a business in their own name, with no legal separation between owner and business.

  • Single owner
  • No incorporation required
  • Owner bears personal liability

Section 8 Company

For charitable, educational or social initiatives — a non-profit company that cannot distribute profits.

  • Non-profit objectives
  • Limited liability
  • No dividend distribution

Not sure which structure fits your situation? Options depend on your goals and circumstances — a registration advisor can help you compare quickly.
