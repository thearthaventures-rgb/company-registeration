How it works

The registration journey

A structured path — the work happens in the right order, and you always know where you stand.

  1. 01

    Business conversation

    Share what you are building — a short call is enough. We listen before we recommend anything.

    In scope: your goals, ownership, timeline
  2. 02

    Structure & name selection

    We compare the structures that could fit your situation and check name availability, so you decide with your eyes open.

    In scope: Private Limited, LLP, OPC & more
  3. 03

    Documentation & DSC

    You receive a tailored checklist and we work through each item with you — including the Digital Signature Certificate digital filing requires.

    In scope: identity proofs, registered office, DSC
  4. 04

    Application preparation

    Your incorporation documents and statutory forms are drafted and internally reviewed before anything is submitted.

    In scope: MOA/AOA & equivalents, statutory forms
  5. 05

    Filing & follow-up

    Applications are submitted, tracked and followed up with responses to any queries — until the registration is complete.

    In scope: submission, tracking, query responses
  6. 06

    Start on the right foot

    You receive your incorporation documents and a clear view of the registrations and compliance ahead.

    In scope: incorporation papers, PAN/TAN direction

Not sure which step applies to you yet? Tell us where you are today and we’ll point you to the right starting point.
