The real work

Why registration takes more than filling a form

Incorporation is a sequence of regulated steps. Each one can stall your application if it isn’t handled carefully — and the gaps show up later.

01

Choosing the wrong structure

Your entity follows your business for years. A mismatch surfaces later — in compliance costs, investor appetite, or the difficulty of restructuring.

02

A name that doesn’t clear

Rejected or disputed names mean rework and delay before your application ever moves forward.

03

Gaps in director & KYC details

Identity, address, consent and appointment details must align with the structure you choose. Errors here are a common review point.

04

Documentation that doesn’t hold up

Incomplete or inconsistent documents are a leading cause of deficiencies and re-submission.

05

Filing mistakes & regulator queries

Errors in applications attract queries that slow the process down — and each round-trip takes time.

06

Stopping at the certificate

PAN, TAN and compliance registrations follow incorporation. Companies that overlook them face penalties.
